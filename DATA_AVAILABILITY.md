# Data Availability

The Source Data underlying the figures and tables are provided in
`results/source_data/` together with machine-readable manifests. The source
code and 30 frozen
model artifacts correspond to version `v1.0.0` in the public GitHub repository
`Grtresy/mayr-nucpred` and the Zenodo record under DOI
[`10.5281/zenodo.22059011`](https://doi.org/10.5281/zenodo.22059011). Public
availability begins when the corresponding release records are published.

The underlying curated Mayr and ESNUEL datasets are not redistributed in this
repository because their records were assembled from third-party literature
and database sources with source-specific reuse and redistribution conditions.
Publisher PDFs and supporting-information files are not included. Original
sources are to be accessed under their own terms and cited as described in the
manuscript and repository metadata.

Additional author-generated derived records may be provided by the
corresponding author, Shaoguang Zhang (`sgzhang@tsinghua.edu.cn`), upon
reasonable request for research and reproducibility purposes, subject to the
licences and redistribution rights of the underlying third-party sources.
Requests will be assessed for whether the requested fields can lawfully be
redistributed; third-party source files themselves will not be supplied unless
their terms expressly permit it.

The code, Source Data, and model weights become publicly available only when
their corresponding repository and release records are published. A saved
GitHub or Zenodo draft does not constitute public availability. Reused public
datasets and literature sources must be cited under their own version and
access metadata.
